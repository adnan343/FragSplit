# Storefront artwork

The homepage hero uses a generated editorial still life of cut bergamot, citrus peel and a glass prism. The decant guide feature uses an illustration of glass atomizers. The catalog uses four generated fragrance-note studies, each bearing the visible label “Demo illustration.” The owner should upload their own product photography and descriptive alternative text for live listings. Each study illustrates a scent association and makes no claim about a perfume's composition, packaging or provenance.

## Provenance and generation prompts

The editorial still life and all five illustrations were generated specifically for this repository using built-in ImageGen. The local WebP exports strip source metadata. Each catalog export includes the disclosure directly in the image.

The bergamot image was generated as a standalone panoramic background with open shadow on the left for live text, an illuminated citrus half on the right and a glass prism on warm stone. A crop of the same artwork supplies the favicon.

The atomizer illustration prompt requested a 3:2 gouache and cut-paper still life of two unmarked travel atomizers with bronze caps and amber liquid, on pale travertine beside a bergamot twig. It specified directional afternoon light, rendered glass reflections, warm cream, amber, ochre and olive, with no lettering, logos, manufacturer packaging or photographic substitution.

Each catalog prompt requested a square botanical reference illustration. Shared direction specified gouache and layered cut paper on matte stock, directional sunlight, a centered composition surrounded by open color and no product packaging, branding or text. Individual subjects were amber resin with a dried labdanum leaf on terracotta, bergamot with a curling peel on celadon, a rose and bud on dusty pink, and cedar curls with cut timber and a pine twig on pale sage.
