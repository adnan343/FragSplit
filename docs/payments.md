# Cash on delivery

VialMuse accepts cash on delivery. The order reserves liquid until the store confirms it or the hold expires. Confirmation consumes the reserved stock and queues the itemized order slip. Record collection after delivery. Record a COD refund after the customer has been reimbursed.

Historical online orders remain visible for records and reporting. SSLCommerz checkout, notifications, reconciliation and online refund controls are out of scope for this release. Reintroducing online payments requires authenticated and rate-limited notifications, deduplicated verification using the provider API, refund records per provider charge and merchant sandbox verification of success, decline or cancellation and IPN delivery.
